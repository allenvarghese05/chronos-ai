# T4.5 — Render job and verification (brief)

Branch `t4-5-render`, stacked on `t4-4-renderer-scenes` (#50).

| ID | Task | Size | Depends | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T4.5 | Render job + verification: `reel_json.py` builder, per-render `public/` staging, `remotion render` runner, `ffprobe` verification, thumbnail, sha256, timing metrics, `caffeinate`; end-to-end `chronos run` | L | T4.3, T4.4 | `@pytest.mark.remotion` end-to-end on the fixture produces a valid MP4; ffprobe assertions; total stage durations logged and under budget table in `07-media-pipeline.md` §5 |

Decisions made overnight under the owner's go-ahead:

1. **Per-render public dir lives in the reel's own folder** (`data/media/<date>/<id>/public`): `voice.wav`, the scene images and a symlink to the renderer fonts. Remotion gets `--public-dir`, so nothing is ever copied into the repo. The dir is removed after each render.
2. **Runs inside the job sandbox.** Remotion goes through `media.subprocess.run` (cwd under `data/`, `PATH` = node's directory only). This was verified to work before building on it.
3. **Retry once at `--concurrency=1`** (docs/02 §7). A failure marks the reel `failed` with the error.
4. **ffprobe verification:** h264 1080×1920 @ 30 fps, aac, and duration within 0.5 s of `(ceil(audio×30)+15)/30`. The thumbnail is a still at frame 45.
5. **Theme.** `auto` gives `dawn` on weekdays and `ink` at weekends; an explicit preference wins. The attribution lists the source names plus the voice and captions credits.
6. **`renderer_version`** = `<packages/renderer version>+MorningReel`.
7. **`caffeinate` is not used per stage.** The laptop's `pmset` schedule and launchd keep it awake during prewarm (T0.6), and render takes ~1 minute. A `caffeinate -i` wrapper would need `/usr/bin/caffeinate` in the sandboxed argv; deferred.
8. **Renderer legibility fix (found in the live render).** Over photos and video the story text now sits on a dark blurred card (78 %), so contrast holds over busy images such as paper screenshots (docs/07 §6).
