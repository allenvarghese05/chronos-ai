| T4.4 | Remotion compositions: design tokens, fonts, `CoverScene`, `StoryScene` (image / b-roll / gradient), `OutroScene`, `WordCaptions`, transitions, themes, `ReelProps` from generated zod; `renderer_version` | L | T0.5, T3.4 | Vitest for timing maths and caption line-breaking; still smoke in CI at frames 0/60/600; visual review of `sample-reel.json` render by the owner (checklist in `12-testing-and-quality.md` §4) |

Decisions made overnight under the owner's go-ahead:

1. **StoryScene.**
   - Media layer: an `Img` with a slow linear Ken Burns zoom (1.00→1.08), a muted `OffthreadVideo` for b-roll, or an animated gradient from the theme palette.
   - Top and bottom scrim for contrast.
   - A kicker chip and a fitted headline (72/64/56 px by length, clamped to 3 lines).
   - Bullets spring in, staggered 8 frames apart. A "via <source>" badge.
2. **WordCaptions.** Lines of 3–5 words (existing `layoutCaptions`). The current line is the last one whose first word has started; the active word is emphasised by weight (700 vs 500) and accent colour, not scale (docs/07 §6). Word times are absolute, so the component gets the scene's start.
3. **Transitions are 12-frame fades** in and out on every scene, rather than `@remotion/transitions`: TransitionSeries overlaps scenes and would shift the audio-locked timings.
4. **Fixture and smoke.** `sample-reel.json` is now a 4-scene reel (cover, 2 stories, outro) with captions. CI renders stills at frames **0/60/600**, and the artifact upload uses `smoke-*.png`.
5. **`renderer_version`** is recorded by the T4.5 render job from `packages/renderer/package.json` (`<version>+MorningReel`).
6. **Visual review by the owner is still pending.** Use `pnpm exec remotion studio src/index.ts` in `packages/renderer` (or the T4.5 MP4).
