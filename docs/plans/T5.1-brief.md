# T5.1 — Media serving and retention (brief)

Branch `t5-1-media-serving`, stacked on `t4-5-render` (#51).

| ID | Task | Size | Depends | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T5.1 | Media serving + retention: signed URLs, `GET /v1/media`, Range support, `GET /v1/reels*`, save/delete, retention job (reels 24 h, telemetry 30 d, items 14 d, jobs), nightly VACUUM backup | M | T4.5 | Expired signature → 410; path traversal attempts → 404 (tests); retention test with frozen clock deletes exactly the expected rows and files |

Decisions made overnight under the owner's go-ahead:

1. **Media URLs.**
   - HMAC-SHA256(secret, `public_id/file|exp`), 1 h expiry, as in docs/05.
   - **File-name whitelist** (`reel.mp4`, `thumb.jpg`, `captions.json`, `voice.mp3`) plus a strict ULID check, so traversal is impossible by construction.
   - Bad signature or unknown file → **404**, which reveals nothing; expired link or retired reel → **410 `media_expired`**.
2. **Range.** A single `bytes=a-b`/`a-`/`-n` range gives 206; malformed or multi-range gives 416 with `Content-Range: bytes */size`. `Cache-Control: private, max-age=0`.
3. **Reels API.** Bearer token, device-scoped (another device's reel is 404). The list holds live plus saved reels, newest first. Saved reels never expire (`expires_at` shown as null). Detail scenes come from the stored `reel.json`. `DELETE` removes the folder, asset rows and the reel row; the brief stays as history.
4. **Retention runs as a job.** It is enqueued by the scheduler once per device-local day after the 03:00 boundary (keyed by date, idempotent). FK-safe order: unsaved reels > 24 h → days/briefs > 90 d not used by any remaining reel → evaluations > 30 d not referenced → telemetry > 30 d not referenced → items > 14 d not in any brief → push > 30 d → jobs done > 7 d / failed > 30 d. All SQL is literal with bound parameters.
5. **Backup.** `VACUUM INTO data/backups/chronos-YYYYMMDD.sqlite`, keeping the newest 7.
